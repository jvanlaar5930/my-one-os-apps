A classic Snake game where players control a snake to eat food and grow while avoiding collisions with walls and itself.

Features:
• Snake movement with arrow key controls and real-time direction changing
• Food generation at random positions that appear when snake eats
• Score tracking that increases by 10 points for each food consumed
• Collision detection for walls, self, and game over conditions
• Restart functionality to begin new games after losing
• Visual grid-based playing field with snake and food elements

Bridge & data:
os.storage.save and os.storage.load for high score persistence
os.window.resize to adjust game area to window size

Layout:
Single full-screen game board with score display at top and controls at bottom
